Our Core Solutions

Specialized Warehouse & Logistics Services

HK Logistics focuses on four core strategic services tailored to support modern commercial operations, enterprise brands, and growing distribution networks.

Pillar 1 • Storage

1. Warehousing

Grade-A pallet storage with selective racking, heavy floor load capacity, climate-controlled chambers, and dedicated clean zones for consumer goods, beauty products, and high-value cargo.

  • Shared multi-client & dedicated storage options
  • Temperature-controlled storage (+15°C to +25°C)
  • Strict inventory barcode scanning & cycle counting
View Warehousing Details →
Pillar 2 • Risk Protection

2. Insurance Services

Comprehensive cargo risk coverage and goods-in-transit protection designed to safeguard client inventory from damage, loss, or transit disruption with straightforward claims handling.

  • Full-value warehouse inventory insurance coverage
  • Transit and freight movement protection policies
  • Rapid claim turnaround & documented chain-of-custody
View Insurance Details →
Pillar 3 • Fulfillment

3. Drop Shipment

Agile direct-to-consumer and B2B order fulfillment. We receive supplier orders, pack directly to client specifications, and dispatch same-day to reduce carrying costs and accelerate turnaround.

  • Rapid pick, pack, and label dispatch operations
  • Seamless order routing without holding excess stock
  • Cross-docking transshipment to major commercial carriers
View Drop Shipment Details →
Pillar 4 • Procurement

4. Sourcing Company

Strategic procurement and sourcing services. We assist clients in locating verified manufacturers, conducting factory-level quality checks, and coordinating reliable inbound logistics directly to our warehouse.

  • Supplier vetting, price negotiation & contract support
  • Pre-shipment batch inspection & sample validation
  • Direct integration with our warehousing and drop ship lines
