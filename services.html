Warehousing & Strategic Inventory Storage

HK Logistics provides flexible, enterprise-grade warehousing designed to meet the rigorous demands of leading cosmetic, beauty, FMCG, and consumer brands.

Whether you require shared multi-client pallet positions to keep costs variable or dedicated storage bays with custom racking, our facilities are equipped with heavy industrial slab floors, ESFR fire suppression, barcode tracking, and 24/7 security.

✓ High-Bay Selective Pallet Racking: Over 45,000 pallet positions with rapid reach-truck retrieval.
✓ Climate-Controlled Chambers: Strict temperature control (+15°C to +25°C) for cosmetics, skincare, and sensitive goods.
✓ Multi-Client Flexibility: Scale pallet capacity up or down seasonally with no long-term lease lock-ins.
✓ Barcode & Cycle Counting: 99.8% confirmed inventory accuracy with regular verification audits.

Comprehensive Insurance Services & Risk Protection

Safeguarding your high-value inventory is central to our partnership. HK Logistics provides comprehensive risk protection covering both warehouse storage and goods in transit.

Supply chains carry inherent risks—from road incidents and weather disruption to handling anomalies. Our insurance programs give brand owners complete peace of mind, ensuring that in the rare event of damage or loss, your capital is fully indemnified.

✓ Full Warehouse Stock Coverage: Protection against fire, water damage, electrical disruption, and facility hazards.
✓ Transit & Cargo-in-Motion Policies: Door-to-door coverage spanning highway freight, air cargo, and container transshipment.
✓ Fast & Transparent Claims Handling: Documented chain of custody with dedicated insurance support for rapid resolution.
✓ High-Value Cargo Endorsements: Specialized policies tailored for luxury personal care, cosmetics, and electronics.

Service Pillar 3

Drop Shipment & Omnichannel Fulfillment

Accelerate delivery speed and eliminate fixed overhead with HK Logistics’ agile drop shipment operations. We connect your sales channels directly to final delivery without unnecessary middle steps.

When orders arrive, our experienced logistics staff pick items with barcode verification, package them in accordance with your brand guidelines, apply compliant shipping labels, and hand over to scheduled transport carriers on the same business day.

✓ Same-Day Order Processing: Swift turnaround from order notification to dock staging and dispatch.
✓ Custom Packaging & Kitting: Secondary boxing, promotional leaflets, shrink wrapping, and custom barcode labels.
✓ Cross-Docking Velocity: Sub-4 hour flow-through for inbound supplier containers routed directly outbound.
✓ Electronic Proof of Delivery: Real-time tracking numbers and signed digital confirmations for every shipment.

Service Pillar 4

Strategic Sourcing & Procurement Services

HK Logistics acts as your trusted sourcing partner, connecting your enterprise with certified manufacturers, conducting rigorous quality checks, and coordinating reliable inbound logistics directly into our warehouse.

Navigating overseas suppliers and quality consistency can be challenging. Our sourcing specialists handle vendor verification, sample validation, commercial negotiation, and container consolidation so your brand receives verified goods on schedule.

✓ Verified Manufacturer Discovery: Pre-screened suppliers audited for production capacity and compliance.
✓ On-Site Quality Control: Pre-shipment batch inspections, formulation checks, and defect elimination.
✓ Container Consolidation & Inbound Freight: End-to-end management of ocean containers and customs clearance.
✓ Direct Warehouse Receiving: Shipments arrive directly at our dock doors for immediate storage and drop-ship readiness.
