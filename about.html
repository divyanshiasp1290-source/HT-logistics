About HK Logistic Sp. z o.o

A recognized corporate warehouse, drop shipment, and distribution partner dedicated to operational excellence, absolute cargo protection, and high-velocity supply chain management.

Company Background

Built on Trust, Security, and Seamless Distribution

HK Logistic Sp. z o.o was established to provide brands, distributors, and growing enterprises with an institutional-grade logistics partner that prioritizes speed, accountability, and cargo security.

We operate modern, multi-client and dedicated warehouse facilities engineered to protect your inventory while significantly lowering operating overheads. Our integrated suite of services spans Grade-A pallet warehousing, full-coverage cargo insurance, fast-turn drop shipment fulfillment, and expert product sourcing.

From high-value personal care and cosmetics to packaged consumer goods, HK Logistic Sp. z o.o protects brand reputation with strict barcode tracking, clean environment protocols, and reliable dispatch execution.

Operating Principles

The Foundation of Our Operational Reliability

Four essential commitments guide every warehouse movement, inventory count, and shipment managed by HK Logistics.

Absolute Custody & Accountability

We treat our clients' inventory as our own. From inbound unloading to outbound staging, every carton is scanned, tracked, and protected under our comprehensive insurance framework.

Agile Execution Speed

Modern commerce cannot afford dispatch bottlenecks. Our drop shipment and cross-docking workflows process orders rapidly to meet demanding retail and consumer expectations.

Grade-A Storage Infrastructure

Our facilities feature super-flat concrete floors, 14m clear ceiling heights, ESFR sprinkler protection, climate monitoring, and 24/7 guarded security access.

Client-First Partnership

We operate with complete commercial transparency. No hidden handling fees, no rigid contracts—just flexible logistics tailored to your exact business cycle.
